Frequently Asked Questions

What homes are available at Codename Hennur?

3 and 4 BHK apartments: 3 BHK of approx. 2,000 sq ft, 3 BHK + S of approx. 2,400 sq ft and 4 BHK + S of approx. 2,700 sq ft.

What is the price?

Pricing is ₹16,200 per sq ft*. That works out to approx. ₹3.24 Cr* for the 3 BHK, ₹3.89 Cr* for the 3 BHK + S and ₹4.37 Cr* for the 4 BHK + S. Prices are indicative and exclude taxes and statutory charges; ask for the price sheet for the exact cost of a unit.

Where is Codename Hennur located?

At Hennur, a stone's throw from the Outer Ring Road in North Bengaluru, with direct access to the ORR and smooth connectivity to Manyata Tech Park and the commercial hubs around it.

How big is the project?

Approx. 200 apartments on 3 acres, in a single tower with two wings rising 3 basements + ground + 24 floors. About 80% of the land is kept as open space.

What makes the apartments different?

Every apartment is planned with a 270-degree open view and luxury specifications, and the project has 20+ amenities.
